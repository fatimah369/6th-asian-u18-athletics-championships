Our Role

What Mood Event delivered on the ground.

Six interlocking deliverables, each owned end-to-end by a dedicated Mood Event team, combined into a single, cohesive championship experience.

Full Event Coordination & On-Site Management

Centralised command across all venues and zones, schedules, briefings, decision-making and cross-team coordination, every day of the championship.

Operations, Logistics & Crowd Organization

Movement of teams, equipment and officials between venues, plus structured crowd flow planning to keep spectators, media and athletes safely separated.

Full Catering Services

Continuous catering for athletes, international and local referees and officials across multiple locations, sustained over all 7 days of operations.

Opening Ceremony Production

Creative direction and full production of the opening show, 70 performers on stage, representing the colours and spirit of all 29 participating nations.

Official Event Host

Mood Event provided the official championship host, leading announcements, ceremonies and on-stage moments in front of athletes, dignitaries and media.

Official Opening Speech Writing

Drafting and refining the official opening speech, calibrated for the championship's audience, sponsors and continental significance.
